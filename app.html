    How it works

    • Click a body part, prop or light to select it. Drag empty space to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
    • Rotate turns the selected joint. Move on a hand or foot bends the whole limb (IK); on the body it moves the hips with the feet planted; the ring on the floor moves the whole figure.
    • Add point and spot lights in Lighting, then place them with Move and aim spots with Rotate.
    • Export gives a clean PNG (no gizmo) at 1×, 2× or 4×, optionally transparent.
