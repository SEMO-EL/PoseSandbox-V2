FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Does PoseSandbox work in the browser?
Yes — it runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone. Nothing to install, no account, and your last scene is kept in the browser between visits.
What’s included in the Starter Pack?
The Starter Pack includes 20 preset poses. No props, no scenes — just clean pose presets you can use right away.
What’s included in the other packs?
They’re in the works. Expect bigger pose libraries and extra workflow goodies — details will be revealed soon.
What does “Open App” do?
It opens app.html, which is the PoseSandbox posing tool.
Will the background affect performance?
No. It is a light canvas animation that pauses when it scrolls out of view or the tab is hidden, and stays still if your system asks for reduced motion. The editor only redraws when something changes.
