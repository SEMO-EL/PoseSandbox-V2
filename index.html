FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Does PoseSandbox work in the browser?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone. Nothing to install, no account, and your last scene is kept in the browser between visits.

What’s in the Starter Pack?

The Starter Pack has 20 preset poses. No props, no scenes: just clean pose presets you can use right away.

What’s in the other packs?

They are in the works. Expect bigger pose libraries and extra workflow tools; details will come soon.

What does “Open the app” do?

It opens app.html, the PoseSandbox posing tool.

Will the 3D figure on this page slow my computer down?

No. It loads after the page, only draws while you can see it, pauses in a hidden tab, and holds still if your system asks for reduced motion. The editor only redraws when something changes.
